Outbreak in the Ozarks

Recent reports of the Ozarks being plagued with a zombie outbreak caused by none other than the vibe coders. Avoid contact with brainless individuals and steer clear of the brain rot. Keeping yourself away from these things could prevent catastrophic problems.

Top 3 Survival Rules

  1. Stay away from high population density areas
  2. Keep your group small and don't pick up randos
  3. Set up camp and brush up on your foraging/hunting

Survival Kit Necessities

Survival Knife

A knife is overall one of the most useful tools in terms of survival. To survive outside without the comfort of your home you are going to need something that is a multipurpose tool a knife solve most issues including creating other tools for survival.

Flint and Steel

It might not be cold out right now, but a flint and steel is the fool proof firestarter. If you have fire you have a lot more than most. Cooking and warmth are the main two advantages of this.

First-Aid Kit

Getting hurt is something that is inevitable whether you scrape yourself hunting or roll your ankle having materials or resources to treat those injuries is very important. Survival isn't pretty and you need to make sure that you take care of any injuries to prevent infections.
